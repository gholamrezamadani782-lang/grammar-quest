GRAMMAR QUEST - offline iPad app
================================

PUT IT ONLINE ONCE (on a computer, about 5 minutes)
1. Go to github.com and sign in. Click "+" > "New repository".
   Name: grammar-quest. Choose Public. Click "Create repository".
2. Click "uploading an existing file". Unzip this zip, open the folder,
   select EVERYTHING inside it (index.html, sw.js, chapters, fonts, icons...)
   and drag it all into the page. Click "Commit changes".
3. Go to Settings > Pages. Under "Branch" choose "main" and "/ (root)". Save.
4. Wait 1-2 minutes. Your address is:
   https://gholamrezamadani782-lang.github.io/grammar-quest/

INSTALL ON THE IPAD (once, with internet)
1. Open that address in Safari (not Chrome).
2. Tap Share (the square with an arrow) > "Add to Home Screen" > Add.
3. Open Grammar Quest from the Home Screen and wait for "Ready to use offline".
4. Done. It now works with no internet. Always open it from the Home Screen.

GOOD TO KNOW
- Progress is saved on the iPad. Settings (gear) > Export backup keeps a copy.
- Apple Pencil: iPad Settings > Apple Pencil > turn on Scribble, then write in the answer box.
- Nicer voice: iPad Settings > Accessibility > Spoken Content > Voices > English.
  Download one, then pick it in the app's Settings.
- New chapter later: upload the new files to the same repository (replace the old ones).
  Open the app once with internet, then close and reopen it.
